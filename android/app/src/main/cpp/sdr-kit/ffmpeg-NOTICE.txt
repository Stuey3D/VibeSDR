FFmpeg in VibeServer
====================

The Linux VibeServer package includes a copy of the FFmpeg command-line program, installed as
/usr/lib/vibeserver/ffmpeg (and, in the armhf package, /usr/lib/vibeserver/armv6/ffmpeg for
processors without NEON). VibeServer runs it as a separate process to decode xHE-AAC (USAC)
audio from DRM stations, and DAB+ audio when the owner chooses FFmpeg instead of the built-in
FAAD2 decoder.
