What the network can hear:
Filter the servers by any of these, alone or together:
  • Frequency — click a band on the band plan (several can be selected), or use the arrows to place the two markers on the dial; one marker asks who can hear that frequency, both mark a range. Zoom with the buttons; on the Full Network Range dial, drag the yellow box to move the Zoomed View.
  • Location — choose a country, several countries, or a whole region.
  • Modes & decoders — the buttons below the dial keep only servers that offer them.
  • A station — type its name below; pick one and the receivers most likely to hear it are ranked.

VibeServer keeps a small store in your browser so your display settings and bookmarks travel between radios and servers. Adjust your display settings in the SDR view and they are saved for that radio; press Save for all there and they become your default on every server in this directory. A radio or server can still keep its own settings if conditions there need them. Nothing leaves your browser.
