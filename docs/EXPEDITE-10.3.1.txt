THE REASON — send this if App Review asks why it is urgent
----------------------------------------------------------
VibeSDR 10.3 has been live since 16 August and contains a defect that leaves
users with no audio at all.

Audio playback startup races with itself: on many connections the app sets up
the audio path and immediately tears it down again, so the signal, the waterfall
and the data meter all look perfect and there is simply no sound. It is
intermittent, there is no reliable workaround, and it affects the app's core
function, which is listening to a radio.

It was reported by a user on both iPad and iPhone (GitHub issue #20) and
reproduced on our own devices.

10.3.1 (build 140) fixes that fault and two related audio failures. There are no
new features and no other changes.

We are requesting an expedited review because every user who updates to 10.3 is
currently left with a silent app, and we have no way to reach them or to roll
the release back.


IF THEY ASK WHAT CHANGED (short version)
----------------------------------------
1. Audio engine start/stop was split across two threads, so a teardown could
   land after the setup it was meant to precede and silence the session.
2. The audio connection could open before the server had registered the session,
   which some servers refuse silently; it now waits, and re-registers if audio
   stalls instead of retrying indefinitely.
3. On a local network address the audio connection could not be established at
   all by one transport; it now falls back to the other automatically.

Nothing outside the audio path was touched.
