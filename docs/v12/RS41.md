## Sources and the house rule

- **Listener documentation only** — community write-ups of the RS41 frame (modulation, the whitening mask, the header,
  the two interleaved Reed–Solomon codewords, the block layout, the calibration subframes and the temperature formula).
  GPL decoders were **not** opened for this work and nothing in the tree is copied from one. No comparison with any
  other decoder is made or implied.
- Our own code everywhere: the RS errors-and-erasures decoder follows our VDL2 one (`vibe_vdl2_fec.h`), the front end
  is vibedsp's Channelizer (as `vibe_avi_front.h`).

### The facts used, and which are unverified

Every one of these is from listener documentation and **unverified on air**. Where a fact is wrong the symptom is
always the same: **real frames are refused** (Reed–Solomon or a CRC fails), never a wrong sonde shown — so the first
capture settles them, and `test-rs41 --cu8` is the tool.

### Not decoded, on purpose

- **Humidity and pressure.** The models we found for them are described by their own authors as empirical fits, not
  the sonde's documented calculation. Showing them would be a guess — the brief's rule is never a guessed PTU. They wait
  for a real capture alongside an independent reading of the same sonde.
- **The temperature appears only with all 51 calibration subframes** (about 51 s of clean frames) and only when it is a
  plausible air temperature (−100…+60 °C). Until then every client says "not yet calibrated (n of 51)". The app's and the
  web's parser also drop a `temp` sent beside an incomplete calibration (`test_sondes_app`).
- Other sonde types: ★ 2026-11-19 the **DFM-06/09/17 and M10/M10+/M20 are now decoded in this same mode** — see
  `SONDES-TYPES.md`. Still not: iMet, RS92, SRS-C50, LMS6; burst-kill / flight-state bytes, the extra-data instruments (ozone).
- **SondeHub upload** — out of scope (spot uploads are parked by Stuart).

## Decision: its own whole-radio mode, in the datalink's shape (not ISM's band scheme)

ISM's bands carry ISM's engine: OOK / FSK device protocols at 433 / 868 / 915 MHz with their own capture rules and gallery.
A sonde is one continuous GFSK signal a second, wants a **map with tracks** and a list by serial, and needs the radio at
400–406 MHz — the shape of AIS and the aircraft datalink, whose BANDS mechanics (`step` clamped at the ends, a centre
between channels so the DC remnant hits nothing, no rate change, the receiver given back as found) fit exactly. So:
**SONDES**, a whole-radio mode copied from the datalink (`avEnter` / `avLeave` / `avTick` / `avControl` → `sd…`).

- **Bands** (`vibe_rs41_service.h`): `lo` 400–402, `mid` 402–404 (the default), `hi` 404–406 MHz, each centred 5 kHz off
  the 10 kHz raster sondes use (401.005 / 403.005 / 405.005); and `all` (400–406, centred 403.005) **only on a radio
  already running ≥ 6.7 MS/s** — the mode never changes the rate. A capture narrower than 2 MHz searches the middle of the
  band (the report's `lo` / `hi` say what).
- **Reach**: not on an Airspy HF+, not on an Icom, not on a radio whose range does not hold a whole band; greyed with the
  reason when the owner switched it off, the centre is locked, or the band asked for needs a wider capture.

## Architecture (`vibe_rs41_rx.h`)

1. **One forward FFT for the whole band** (vibedsp Channelizer, overlap-save, PFFFT; bins ≤ 600 Hz: 4096 points at
   2.4 MS/s). Half-band decimators first only where the capture is much wider than the band (the `all` band on 10 MS/s).
2. **The scan**: each bin's power smoothed over ~1 s (`powerEma`, NEON / SSE2 / scalar); twice a second, peaks in a 10 kHz
   box 2 dB over the median floor that are **not a carrier** (< 60 % of the excess in the middle three bins — the survey's
   constant spurs) and **not a broad block** (each 10 kHz flank < 40 % — the survey's 240 kHz block at 402.6 MHz). The
   centroid is the channel's frequency.
3. **The channels**: each candidate is a 32-bin slice of the same bins (18.75 kS/s at 2.4 MS/s: flat to ±4.7 kHz),
   inverse-transformed, a sub-bin NCO. Up to 6 at once (`sdMaxChannels`); one that has decoded nothing for 20 s gives its
   slot up (parked a minute) when another candidate is waiting; closed after 15 s unseen and unheard; re-centred on each
   frame's measured frequency error.
4. **The demodulator** per channel: FM discriminator (`fmDisc`, a SIMD atan2 — A&S 4.4.49 folded to the circle, ≤ 1.2e-5
   rad, NEON / SSE2 / scalar) → linear resampling to exactly 8 samples a bit → a one-bit integrator → the header searched
   at all eight phases by shift register + popcount (no soft work on noise) → the best phase by soft correlation →
   amplitude, offset and SNR by least squares on the header's 64 known bits → bits with an early–late timing nudge →
   de-whiten → Reed–Solomon, errors only, then the weakest 4…20 bytes erased → blocks by CRC.
5. **A sonde exists only when a frame proves it**: both codewords clean, the status block's CRC, a plausible serial.

**The service** (`vibe_rs41_service.h`): its own thread (decoder priority), fed by copy; more than half a second queued
(at most 2 M samples, 16 MB) loses the oldest block and counts it. **The table**: one row per serial (never per
frequency), 64 rows, 400 track points each (a point every 10 s or 30 m / 50 m move, halved when full), the report's track
thinned to 120 points ending on the newest.

**Memory** (256 MB boards exist): the queue ≤ 16 MB (≈ 9.6 MB at 2.4 MS/s); the receiver ≈ 50 KB + ≈ 12 KB a channel;
the table ≤ 64 × (816 B calibration + 400 × 24 B track) ≈ 0.7 MB.

## Wired

- **Server**: `{"type":"sondes", on, band?|step?}` → `{"type":"sondes", band, bandName, hz, lo, hi, dropped,
  chans:[{hz,db,n}], frames, sondes:[{serial, kind, hz, txHz?, age, n, frame, snr, cal, enc?, batt?, gpsTime?, lat, lon,
  alt, climb, speed, heading, sats, posAge, maxAlt, temp?, track:[[lat,lon,alt]…]}], gain, gainAuto}` once a second,
  `sondes_off`, `sondes_error{why}`. hwinfo / vibeserver.json `sondes`, `sondesWhy`, `sondesBands`; in `modes`; the
  datalink's gain rule (85 % up the radio's list, `vibeism::GainRule` on clipping); one whole-radio mode at a time; tune /
  zoom / mode held; leaves when the radio empties and comes back for the same listener (`mode_resume`, with its band — wired as the
  datalink's, **not exercised by a test**); the
  decoders-behind watch; `snr` is the header's eye SNR (the integrated discriminator), not an RF SNR.
- **Owner switch** `sondes` on the setup page and ServerModeScreen (one list); `vibe_radio_caps.h` `sondes`.
- **Benchmark row** `dec_rs41` (primary for `sondes`: below green the switch starts off on a fresh setup, never hidden):
  three sondes in a 2 MHz band at 2.4 MS/s, in real time, scored by the hottest thread. **0.71 % on this Mac (M5)**.
- **Web page**: SONDES in the mode menu and the whole-radio keys; a full-screen TrackView (the bands in the top bar, the
  list, a balloon per sonde — orange and pointing down when falling — a chosen sonde's card and track, EXIT SONDES).
- **App**: SONDES under TUNES THE RADIO ITSELF (a balloon glyph, `modeIcons.ts`); `SondesPanel` (band keys, MAP, EXIT);
  MapOverlay `sondes` on the shared track layer with the chosen sonde's track from the report. Not on Apple TV (no map).
- **R36S**: the `sondes` map mode (list first, the band keys from `sondesBands`, the APRS balloon symbol in the sonde's
  colour, a card), and a canned feed (`VIBE_HHUI_MAP_CANNED=sondes`). Compiled (`-fsyntax-only` on `main.cpp` in the
  `vibe-hh-map:trixie` arm64 image); **not run on the handheld**, no golden; no track lines (the R36S map draws none for
  any mode).
- **Copy**: About V12 note, `sdrTour` (the mode card), the web tutorial.

## Results (all synthetic)

`test-rs41` (124 / 0), one sonde, 2.4 MS/s, 12 s; SNR = signal over the noise in 10 kHz. The first second of every sonde
goes to the scan, so "11 of 12" is every frame after it was found:

| SNR | 4 dB | 6 dB | 7 dB | 8 dB | 9–30 dB |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| frames | 0 | 2 | 11 | 11 | 11 |

- Wrong frames: **0** in every run. Noise for 60 s with three carriers and a random-bit 4800 bit/s GFSK burster: **0
  frames**; 10 s of plain noise opens **no channel**.
- 9 of 10 (all after the first) at −812 / +640 kHz in the band, 3 kHz from the radio's centre, ±1.6 and ±3.2 kHz
  deviation, ±150 ppm bit clock, a carrier left on between frames, 518-byte frames; four sondes at once (12–25 dB) 11 of
  12 each; seven sondes and six channels: six decoded, none wrong; 2.048 MS/s and 1 MS/s.
- Reed–Solomon: 0–12 errors corrected 279 / 279 per length, 13 errors never accepted as a wrong word; 24 erasures and
  16 erasures + 4 errors corrected.
- SIMD: the NEON build (arm64) and `VIBE_FORCE_SCALAR` pass the same suite; the SSE2 path (x86-64, GCC 12, Debian bookworm under
  Docker's amd64 emulation) passes 123 of 124 — the discriminator 1.15e-5 rad, the same SNR / sonde / rate results — the one
  failure being §11's real-time budget (0.283 of real time under emulation, not a measure of an x86 machine). armhf (32-bit
  NEON) not built.
- CPU: 0.005 s per capture second for four sondes at 2.4 MS/s, one thread, on the M5 Pro. **Not measured on a Pi 2** —
  expect the front end to dominate as ACARS's does (one 4096-point FFT a block, no half-bands); measure it with the qemu
  method of `AVIATION.md §Cost` before quoting a figure.
- `test-rs41-service` 42 / 0, `test-server-sondes.mjs` 25 / 0, `test-web-sondes.mjs` 20 / 0 (desktop + 390 px),
  `test_sondes_app.ts` 21 / 0; unchanged: `test-server-avdata` 29 / 0, `test-server-decoder-reach` 35 / 0,
  `test-radio-caps` 241 / 0, `test-setup-radio-caps` 71, `test-bench-defaults`, `test-bench-decoders` (with `dec_rs41`).

   lists the UK sites seen in public sources — unverified); a radio with a **UHF aerial** (both survey radios had HF loops)
