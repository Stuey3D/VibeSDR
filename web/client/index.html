Off — audio always passes.

  

  
  


  
  




  
  
  




    
    

  

  

    Off, audio is compressed to around 10 KB/s. On, you get the raw stream — about 187 KB/s,
    roughly twenty times more, out of the operator's connection. Worth it on a fast link if
    you can hear the difference; a courtesy to leave off if you can't.

WATERFALL

  
  
  
  

    
    

      
      
      
    

  

  
How fast the waterfall scrolls on screen. Higher scrolls smoother, but paired with
    a low data rate it fills the gaps by interpolation — smooth motion, less real detail.

  
  

    
    

      
      
      
      
    

  

  
How many real frames per second the server sends. Lower cuts the data used but can
    miss brief signals. Auto adapts to the link, up to your chosen speed.
